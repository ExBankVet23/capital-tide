## Data & accuracy

Capital Tide runs on a mix of live data from free public sources (FRED, Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, CFTC, Binance, DefiLlama, Alternative.me, AAII, and Cboe) and synthetic data for the small number of series with no free equivalent available. The exact current split is shown transparently in the dashboard's **Data Provenance** panel at all times.
