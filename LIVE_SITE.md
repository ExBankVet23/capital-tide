# 🌊 Try Capital Tide live

The live dashboard is here:

**[https://exbankvet23.github.io/capital-tide/liquidity_flow_dashboard.html](https://exbankvet23.github.io/capital-tide/liquidity_flow_dashboard.html)**

Feel free to have a look around — it's fully open, no signup, no paywall. Explore Core Desk, PRO/MAX, Compare, and Evaluation, and turn on **Flow Guide** (top right) to get a plain-English explanation of anything you hover over.

A quick reminder before you dive in: this is an educational and research project — a demonstration of how much macro data is genuinely reconstructable from free, public sources. It's not built for traders or investors, and nothing on the site is financial advice. Full detail in [`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md) or the in-app Legal & Disclaimer section.

If it's useful or interesting to you, there's a completely optional ☕ Support section in the dashboard. Genuinely never required — just there for anyone who wants to.

Questions, bugs, or ideas? See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
